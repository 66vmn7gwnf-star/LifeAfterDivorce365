# Life After Divorce 365

A WIY concept website for a future guidance, coaching, or educational platform focused on life after divorce.

## Concept direction
The website is intentionally calm, reflective, and practical. It frames the experience as a 365-day journey rather than promising a fixed healing timeline.

## Files
- `index.html`
- `style.css`
- `script.js`
- `assets/sunset-reflection.png` — user-provided image

## Important product note
This is a prototype. It should not present itself as licensed mental-health treatment unless a qualified professional/business is actually providing those services.

The final business model could include:
- guided 365-day programs
- journaling/reflection tools
- coaching
- licensed therapist/counselor directory or referrals
- financial/legal professional resources
- community/support groups
- paid courses or memberships

Before production, any professional claims, privacy policy, crisis resources, intake forms, payment processing, and clinical/legal/financial language should be reviewed by the appropriate qualified professionals.
