365 DAYS ONE STEP AT A TIME
A DIFFERENT WAY FORWARD

You don't have to
have it all figured out.

Divorce can change the life you thought you were building. The next chapter doesn't have to be figured out overnight.

Life After Divorce 365 is a guidance concept designed to help people slow down, understand where they are, and begin building a life that feels like their own again.

THE 365-DAY JOURNEY

Four seasons of
moving forward.

Healing isn't a straight line. The 365 approach gives each season a different focus without forcing a timeline on anyone.

01 / DAYS 1–90

Stabilize

Make space for what happened. Establish routines, boundaries, and a foundation for the next chapter.

02 / DAYS 91–180

TODAY'S REFLECTION

DAY 001

What would you do
if you weren't afraid?

You don't need to answer everything today. Start with one honest question. Write down whatever comes to mind—without judging it.

PRACTICAL GUIDANCE

Rebuilding is
practical, too.

The next chapter isn't only about emotions. It's also about figuring out the everyday pieces of a new life.

01

Identity

Divorce can change the way you see yourself. Give yourself room to discover what matters to you independently of the relationship you left.

  • What parts of yourself do you want to reconnect with?
  • What do you want your days to feel like?
  • What is something you've always wanted to try?
